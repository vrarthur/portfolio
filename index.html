About Me Sobre Mim Sobre Mí À Propos de Moi

I'm a Brazilian editor and filmmaker born in Rio de Janeiro with 2 years of professional experience in Editing, Post-Production, Sound Design, Color Grading, Screenwriting, Translation, Subtitling, Motion Graphics, and Acting. I am currently studying Cinema and Business at PUC-Rio, and what drives me most today is the pursuit of new connections, new challenges, and the desire to perceive and understand the world through a cinematic perspective, language, and aesthetic.

I'm drawn to the world of sports as a cultural phenomenon, in documentary filmmaking, and in stories that explore and spark reflections on current social and political dynamics. I believe every project is a space for experimentation, one that allows me to refine my technique and discover new ways of telling stories. My energy is focused on expanding horizons, especially within the production sector, and collaborating with creative people to transform ideas into collective audiovisual experiences.

Tenho interesse pelo universo dos esportes como fenômeno cultural, pelo formato documental e por histórias que provocam reflexão sobre dinâmicas sociais e políticas atuais.
